Cash On Command

The 3-Story Sequence that turns your Instagram followers into affiliate commissions in 5–10 minutes flat.

You have followers. You have attention. But how do you turn that into cash on demand whenever you need it?

This is the 3-Story Sequence — a simple 3-step story formula you can use anytime you want to make $50–$200 in minutes. Not by begging for sales. Not by being “salesy.” By telling a story that makes people want to click.

All you need is an affiliate product from Clickbank or Digistore24, your Instagram Stories, and this formula.
The Formula

The 3-Story Sequence

Post these 3 stories back-to-back. The whole thing takes under 5 minutes. Here's the exact framework:

📱 Your Instagram Stories
STORY 1

🎣 The Hook

Post something that stops the scroll — a funny meme, a shocking stat, a relatable struggle, or a before/after. It has NOTHING to do with selling yet. Its only job is to get eyeballs and make people tap forward.

STORY 2

📖 The Story-Sell

Now bridge into a quick personal story or testimonial that connects to the product. “I used to struggle with X… then I found this…” Make it emotional and relatable. Don't name the product yet. Build curiosity.

STORY 3

💸 The CTA

Reveal the solution. “Link in bio” or use the link sticker. Make it urgent: “Only available today” / “Limited spots.” Tell them EXACTLY what to do: “Tap the link in my bio right now.”

Deep Dive

Story 1: The Hook — In Detail

The hook story is the most important. If nobody watches it, stories 2 and 3 don't matter.

1
What Makes a Great Hook Story

Your hook needs to do ONE thing: make them want to see the next story. Here are the best types:

  • Funny/relatable meme about money struggles, hustling, or daily life
  • Shocking stat — “Did you know 78% of people live paycheck to paycheck?”
  • Controversial opinion — “9-to-5 jobs are a scam. Here's why…”
  • Before/after result — screenshot of $0 vs. $500 day (use the product's testimonials)
  • Poll or question sticker — “Would you quit your job if you made $200/day online?”
Key RuleDo NOT mention anything about a product, a link, or buying anything in Story 1. It's pure attention-grabbing. The sale happens later.

Story 2: The Story-Sell — In Detail

This is where you bridge from entertainment to interest. You're planting a seed without pushing a sale.

2
How to Write the Story-Sell

Use this simple 3-sentence framework:

  • The Problem: “I used to [struggle they relate to]…”
  • The Discovery: “Then I found [vague hint at solution]…”
  • The Result: “Now I [desirable outcome]…”
✍️ Example Script

“I used to stress about bills every single month. Then someone showed me this simple system that takes 20 minutes a day. Last week I made $347 while watching Netflix. I'll show you in the next story…”

You can use text overlay on a selfie, a text-only story with background color, or a video of yourself talking. Authenticity converts better than perfection.

Pro MoveUse the product's actual testimonials or case studies as your “story.” You don't have to make money with the product yet — share OTHER people's results.

Story 3: The CTA — In Detail

This is the money story. Direct, clear, urgent.

3
How to Close the Sale
  • Name the product or describe it: “this free training” / “this $7 guide”
  • Link sticker or “Link in bio” — make it OBVIOUS where to go
  • Add urgency: “Only 50 spots left” / “Price goes up tonight” / “I can only share this today”
  • Tell them exactly what to do: “Tap the link in my bio RIGHT NOW before it's gone”
✍️ Example CTA

“If you want to learn the exact system — there's a free training in my bio link. It won't be free forever and spots are filling up. Tap my profile → link in bio → watch the training. Do it NOW while it's still up.”

Timing MattersPost your 3-Story Sequence when your audience is most active. Check your Instagram Insights → “Most Active Times.” For most niches: 8–10am and 7–9pm work best.

Where to Get Your Affiliate Product

  • Go to the Marketplace and filter by your niche
  • Look for products with high gravity (Clickbank) or high conversion rate (Digistore24)

Repeat & Scale

How to Use This Again & Again

★
Making it a Habit

The beauty of the 3-Story Sequence is you can use it whenever you need cash. But here are rules to keep it effective:

  • Don't do it every day. 2–3 times per week max. You don't want to burn your audience.
  • Rotate your hooks. Different memes, different stats, different polls each time.
  • Test different products. Some products convert better than others with YOUR audience.
  • Track what works. Screenshot your link clicks after each sequence. Double down on the hooks and stories that get the most taps.
  • Stack with Reels. Post a Reel the same day as your sequence — it pushes more people to your Stories.

If you have 500+ engaged followers and run this sequence 2–3 times a week, you can realistically generate $50–$200+ per sequence depending on the product commission and your audience's trust level.

Step by Step

How to Repost Without Extra Work

📥
Step 1
Download Your Content Watermark-Free

After posting a Reel on Instagram, download it without the watermark. Use apps like SnapInsta, SaveInsta, or SnapSave (all free). You can also save from your drafts before posting.

For TikTok reposts: use SnapTik or SaveTT to download without the TikTok watermark if you create there first.

Why No Watermarks?Platforms punish content with competitor watermarks. A Reel with a TikTok logo on Instagram will get 30–50% less reach. Always remove watermarks before cross-posting.
📝
Step 2
Adapt Your Captions & Hashtags

Each platform has different rules. Quick cheat sheet:

  • Instagram: 15–20 hashtags, longer captions with CTA, link in bio
  • TikTok: 3–5 hashtags, short punchy caption, link in bio or TikTok Shop
  • YouTube Shorts: Title-style caption (searchable), 3–5 hashtags, link in channel description
  • Facebook Reels: Conversational caption, 1–3 hashtags, link in comments or profile

Same video. Different packaging. Takes under 2 minutes per platform.

⏰
Step 3
Stagger Your Posting Times

Don't post on all 4 platforms at the exact same time. Stagger them:

  • Morning (8–10am): Post on Instagram first
  • Midday (12–2pm): Upload to TikTok
  • Afternoon (3–5pm): Upload to YouTube Shorts
  • Evening (7–9pm): Share on Facebook Reels

This way you're getting engagement all day across all platforms instead of one spike.

🔗
Step 4
Set Up Affiliate Links on Every Platform

Every platform needs a way for viewers to click your affiliate link:

  • Instagram: Linktree in bio (you already have this)
  • TikTok: Same Linktree in bio (add once you hit 1K followers, or use the “website” field)
  • YouTube: Add your Linktree to your channel description — available from day one
  • Facebook: Add link in your Page's About section and pin a post with the link

One Linktree. All platforms. Every viewer can find your offer no matter where they discover you.

The $30K System

$30,000 in 30 days. Starting from scratch. Emma's exact day-by-day roadmap.

🔥 4 weeks • Day-by-day actions • From zero to $30K
Emma: I tried everything — affiliate marketing, dropshipping, freelancing. I was $4,000 in credit card debt. Then I stopped doing everything and focused on ONE system. One offer. One traffic source. One audience. In 30 days I generated $30,000 in actual profit. This is the exact roadmap.
1
Days 1–7

Week 1: The Foundation

Most people fail because they skip this. You won't. Build something worth buying first.

DAY 1–2
Choose Your Offer

Your offer must solve a PAINFUL problem for a HUNGRY audience. The three markets that never stop spending: health, wealth, and relationships.

If you're inside Unicorn, you already have a proven offer. If not, go to Clickbank Marketplace and sort by gravity. Find a product with gravity 50+ in your niche. Look for products that use a video sales letter (VSL) — these convert the best.

What to look forHigh commission ($50–$200+), a professional sales page, good reviews, and recurring commissions if possible. One high-converting offer is worth more than ten mediocre ones.
DAY 3–4
Build Your Funnel

You need exactly 3 pages:

  • Capture page: Headline that calls out their pain + email opt-in. “Free training: How I made $500/day in 30 days from scratch.” Keep it simple. One headline, one button.
  • Bridge page: Short video or text that builds trust. Introduce yourself, share a quick story, pre-sell the offer. “Before you watch this training, here's what changed for me…”
  • Thank you page: Redirect to the affiliate offer. Set expectations: “Check your email for more tips.”

Use Systeme.io (free) or ClickFunnels to build this. Ugly funnels that convert beat pretty funnels that don't. Don't spend 3 days on design.

DAY 5–7
Write Your Email Sequence

Write 7 emails that go out over 14 days. Here's the exact framework for each:

  • Email 1 (Day 0): Welcome + deliver what you promised. Link to the offer.
  • Email 2 (Day 1): Your personal story. How you found the product. Relatable struggle.
  • Email 3 (Day 3): A result or testimonial. Social proof that it works.
  • Email 4 (Day 5): Common objections. “I know what you're thinking…” Overcome them.
  • Email 5 (Day 7): More value. Teach something useful. Build trust.
  • Email 6 (Day 10): Urgency. Price going up, spots closing, bonus expiring.
  • Email 7 (Day 14): Final push. Last chance. Direct CTA.

Every email = Story + Lesson + Call to Action. Write like you're texting a friend, not writing a corporate memo.

2
Days 8–14

Week 2: The Traffic Engine

Funnel built. Emails loaded. Now drive traffic like your income depends on it — because it does.

DAY 8–9
Set Up Your Traffic Source

Choose ONE primary platform. For most offers, short-form video is king right now: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.

Create your account. Optimize your bio with a clear CTA and your Linktree. Set up a content calendar: 2–3 posts per day minimum. Batch creating saves time — don't post one at a time.

Platform pick cheat sheetMotivation/biz-opp niche → TikTok or IG. Health/fitness → IG or YouTube. Relationships/dating → TikTok. Over-40 audience → Facebook. Pick ONE and go all in.
DAY 10–12
Content Creation Sprint

Block out 2–3 hours. Film 15–20 short videos in one session. Use this formula for every single one:

  • HOOK (first 2 seconds): Pattern interrupt. Question, bold claim, or visual shock. “I made $347 yesterday doing this one thing…”
  • STORY (5–10 seconds): Quick relatable struggle or insight. “I was broke, working 60 hours, hating my life…”
  • VALUE (5–10 seconds): Teach ONE specific thing. One tip. One hack. One insight.
  • CTA (3 seconds): “Link in bio for the full training.” / “Follow for more.”

Schedule them to post 2–3 per day over the next week. Use the Cash Multiplier strategy to repost on all 4 platforms.

DAY 13–14
Launch Paid Traffic

Even $5–$10/day in ads can change everything. The combo of organic + paid is where the magic happens.

  • Boost your best-performing organic content as ads — it already has social proof
  • Target your niche audience by interest (personal development, make money online, entrepreneurship)
  • Send ad traffic to your capture page, NOT directly to the affiliate offer
  • Start with $5/day. After 3 days, kill anything with cost-per-lead above $3. Scale winners to $10–$20/day
3
Days 15–21

Week 3: The Conversion Machine

Leads are flowing in. Now turn them into buyers.

DAY 15–17
Analyze & Optimize Your Funnel

Look at your numbers. Data tells you exactly where the leaks are:

  • Opt-in rate below 25%? → Rewrite your headline. Test a different pain point.
  • Email open rate below 30%? → Change your subject lines. Use curiosity, numbers, or questions.
  • Click-through rate below 5%? → Rewrite your CTAs. Be more direct. Add urgency.
  • People clicking but not buying? → Improve your bridge page. Add a video testimonial or your own story.
DAY 18–19
Add Social Proof & Urgency

Add testimonials, results screenshots, and case studies to your bridge page and emails. If you don't have your own yet, use the product vendor's testimonials (with permission). Real urgency compresses decisions: limited-time bonuses, countdown timers, closing enrollment dates.

DAY 20–21
The Personal Touch

Personally reach out to your warmest leads — the ones who clicked your link but didn't buy. Send a DM, a voice note, or a 30-second personalized video. This is the highest-converting activity you can do and it costs zero dollars. People buy from people they trust. A personal message fast-tracks that trust.

4
Days 22–30

Week 4: The Scale Explosion

This is where the $30K happens. You have the system. Now pour gasoline on the fire.

DAY 22–24
Double Down on Winners

By now you know which content gets views, which ads have the lowest cost-per-lead, and which emails drive the most clicks. STOP doing everything else. Create more content in the style of your top performers. Increase budget on winning ads. Send more emails similar to your highest-converting ones.

DAY 25–27
Expand Across Platforms

Use the Cash Multiplier strategy: take your winning content and blast it across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels. Each new platform = a new stream of leads pouring into your already-optimized funnel. If your Facebook ads convert, test similar audiences on TikTok Ads.

DAY 28–30
The Sprint Finish

The final push. Go ALL in. More content. Higher ad spend. Extra emails. Flash sale or bonus that expires at midnight on Day 30. This sprint typically generates 40–50% of total revenue. Don't coast at the finish line.

$100/sale × 10 sales/day × 30 days = $30,000

2% funnel conversion? You need 500 visitors/day. Build it. Drive traffic. Optimize. Scale.

How do I get started with Unicorn?
Start by watching the Introduction module in the sidebar — specifically "Platinum Members: Watch This First." It walks you through your full access, what is unlocked, and what to do in your first 24 hours inside the system.
How do I access my affiliate links?
Your affiliate links are inside your JVZoo dashboard. Log in at jvzoo.com, go to "My Purchases", click the Unicorn product, and find your unique affiliate link. You can also access the affiliate center directly from your JVZoo account to generate tracking links.
Can I promote Unicorn as an affiliate?
Yes! As a Platinum member you can promote Unicorn and earn commissions on every sale you refer. Your affiliate link is in your JVZoo dashboard. Module 2 covers exactly how to drive traffic to your links effectively.
When and how do I get paid?
Commissions are processed through JVZoo. Most payments are instant or within 30 days depending on your account standing. You can receive payments via PayPal or direct bank transfer. Make sure your payment details are fully set up in your JVZoo account settings before making sales.
How does the Traffic Pool work?
The Traffic Pool is a shared traffic system where members contribute and receive traffic in return. The more active you are in the system, the more traffic is directed toward your offers. Watch the dedicated Traffic Pool lesson in the sidebar for a full video walkthrough.
Why is a commission showing as pending?
Pending commissions are normal — JVZoo holds commissions for a short period to protect against chargebacks. Once the hold period passes (typically 30 days for new sellers), they will be released automatically. You can check your commission status inside your JVZoo dashboard under "Sales."
My lesson progress is not saving. What do I do?
Progress is saved in your browser's local storage. Make sure you are not in private or incognito mode, and that cookies are enabled in your browser settings. If the issue persists, try clearing your browser cache and reloading the page. Note: progress is stored per device, so switching to a different device will show a fresh state.
The video is not playing. How do I fix it?
First, check your internet connection. If that is fine, try disabling any ad blockers or browser extensions that may block Vimeo content. You can also try opening the members area in a different browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended). If the issue continues, submit a support ticket so we can investigate.
Can I access the members area on my phone?
Yes, the Unicorn members area is fully mobile responsive. Use the menu icon in the top-left corner to open the course sidebar on smaller screens. For the best experience, we recommend using Chrome on Android or Safari on iOS.
What if I need a refund?
Refund requests are handled through JVZoo. Log in, go to "My Purchases", find the Unicorn product, and click "Request Refund." Alternatively, open a support ticket and our team will assist you directly. Please review our refund policy timeframe before submitting a request.
I was charged but cannot access the product. What do I do?
First, check your email (including spam) for your JVZoo receipt and access link. If you still cannot find it, log in to your JVZoo account at jvzoo.com and check "My Purchases." If the product is missing, submit a support ticket with your order number and we will resolve it within 24 hours.
