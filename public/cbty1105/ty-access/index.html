How do I get started with Unicorn?
Start by watching the Introduction module in the sidebar — specifically "Platinum Members: Watch This First." It walks you through your full access, what is unlocked, and what to do in your first 24 hours inside the system.
How do I access my affiliate links?
Your affiliate links are inside your JVZoo dashboard. Log in at jvzoo.com, go to "My Purchases", click the Unicorn product, and find your unique affiliate link. You can also access the affiliate center directly from your JVZoo account to generate tracking links.
Can I promote Unicorn as an affiliate?
Yes! As a Platinum member you can promote Unicorn and earn commissions on every sale you refer. Your affiliate link is in your JVZoo dashboard. Module 2 covers exactly how to drive traffic to your links effectively.
When and how do I get paid?
Commissions are processed through JVZoo. Most payments are instant or within 30 days depending on your account standing. You can receive payments via PayPal or direct bank transfer. Make sure your payment details are fully set up in your JVZoo account settings before making sales.
How does the Traffic Pool work?
The Traffic Pool is a shared traffic system where members contribute and receive traffic in return. The more active you are in the system, the more traffic is directed toward your offers. Watch the dedicated Traffic Pool lesson in the sidebar for a full video walkthrough.
Why is a commission showing as pending?
Pending commissions are normal — JVZoo holds commissions for a short period to protect against chargebacks. Once the hold period passes (typically 30 days for new sellers), they will be released automatically. You can check your commission status inside your JVZoo dashboard under "Sales."
My lesson progress is not saving. What do I do?
Progress is saved in your browser's local storage. Make sure you are not in private or incognito mode, and that cookies are enabled in your browser settings. If the issue persists, try clearing your browser cache and reloading the page. Note: progress is stored per device, so switching to a different device will show a fresh state.
The video is not playing. How do I fix it?
First, check your internet connection. If that is fine, try disabling any ad blockers or browser extensions that may block Vimeo content. You can also try opening the members area in a different browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended). If the issue continues, submit a support ticket so we can investigate.
Can I access the members area on my phone?
Yes, the Unicorn members area is fully mobile responsive. Use the menu icon in the top-left corner to open the course sidebar on smaller screens. For the best experience, we recommend using Chrome on Android or Safari on iOS.
What if I need a refund?
Refund requests are handled through JVZoo. Log in, go to "My Purchases", find the Unicorn product, and click "Request Refund." Alternatively, open a support ticket and our team will assist you directly. Please review our refund policy timeframe before submitting a request.
I was charged but cannot access the product. What do I do?
First, check your email (including spam) for your JVZoo receipt and access link. If you still cannot find it, log in to your JVZoo account at jvzoo.com and check "My Purchases." If the product is missing, submit a support ticket with your order number and we will resolve it within 24 hours.
